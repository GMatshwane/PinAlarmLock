PIN Alarm Lock privacy policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

PIN Alarm Lock (“the app”) is an on-device PIN gate for apps you enrol on the same phone. This policy describes what the app stores and what it does not collect.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Android app with package name com.pinalarmlock.app, distributed as PIN Alarm Lock.

Data the app stores on the device

The app stores the following only in on-device preferences (Android DataStore). None of this is transmitted off the device by the app:

  • A random salt and a SHA-256 hash of your PIN. The PIN itself is not stored.
  • The package names of apps you enrol for protection.

The unlock session exists only in memory. It is cleared when the screen turns off or the app process dies.

Data the app does not collect

  • No internet permission. The app cannot send data to a server.
  • No account, advertising ID, analytics, crash reporting, or location collection.
  • No sale or sharing of installed-app inventory, PIN material, or usage events.

Sensitive permissions

Usage access is used only to see which app is in the foreground so an enrolled app can be covered by the PIN pad. Display-over-other-apps is used only to show that PIN pad. The special-use foreground service keeps that protection running after you leave the app. Notification, vibration, and boot-completed permissions support that same on-device lock function.

The app lists launchable apps with a targeted launcher/home query. It does not request the QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES permission.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
